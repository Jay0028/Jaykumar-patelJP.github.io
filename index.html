The challenge

Modern hydraulic systems
store & reuse energy.

ESR circuits — like accumulators — capture energy during braking or lowering loads and feed it back into the next cycle. Measuring them fairly requires a fundamentally new metric.

Input energy

From the prime mover driving the actuator through the full operating cycle

Recoverable energy

Captured from the load by ESR circuits and stored for the next cycle

System losses

Friction, leakage and heat dissipated across every phase of the cycle

Traditional metrics only see part of this picture — making systems with energy recovery look identical on paper to those without it.

The solution

Cyclic Performance
Index

One formula. Every energy flow. A single number that tells the whole story.

Methodology

Validated in
simulation

MATLAB & Simulink  ·  Electro-Hydrostatic Actuators (EHA)

01

Build EHA model

Developed a dynamic simulation in MATLAB & Simulink capturing all mechanical, hydraulic, and fluid interactions in the actuator system

02

Two configurations

Tested standard EHA vs ESR-integrated EHA side by side under identical operating conditions for a clean direct comparison

03

Measure all energy flows

Input energy, recoverable load energy and all system losses tracked and logged across the complete operating cycle

04

Compute & compare CPI

CPI formula applied to both configurations — producing a clear numerical measure of the performance benefit delivered by ESR integration
